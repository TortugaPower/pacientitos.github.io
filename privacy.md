# Privacy Policy
###### Version 1.0.0 of this document was created on September 16, 2026.

Pacientitos is a clinical photo record-keeping tool. It holds photographs of
patients — sensitive information — and it is built so that this information never
passes through our hands.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected and why. It applies to
the iOS and iPadOS application.

## What information do you collect about me?

None.

Pacientitos has no servers of ours, no user accounts, no analytics, and no
third-party components. We do not receive, and cannot see, any information from your
practice.

## Where are the photographs and patient records stored?

In two places, both under your control:

- **On your device**, inside the app's private container.
- **In your own iCloud account**, in the private CloudKit database belonging to your
  Apple Account.

That private database belongs to your account, not ours. We have no access to it and
cannot inspect its contents, and the space it uses counts against your iCloud storage
plan.

Syncing uses Apple's infrastructure. Apple's handling of that information is governed
by their privacy policy, not by this one.

## Why does the app ask for camera access?

To take the photographs — it is the only way to add images to the app. Pacientitos
does not read your photo library and does not save images into it. When you share a
photo set, the system share sheet is used and you choose the destination.

## Who is responsible for the patient data?

You are. Pacientitos is a tool you use in your practice; the information you record
in it belongs to you and your patients. Obtaining the consent needed to photograph a
patient, and complying with the health and data-protection rules that apply to you,
is your responsibility as a professional.

## Do you share my information?

No. There is nothing to share, because we receive nothing.

## How do I delete the information?

- **A patient and all their photo sets:** delete them inside the app.
- **Everything stored on the device:** delete the app.
- **What is stored in iCloud:** from Settings → your name → iCloud, by managing the
  app's storage.

Because we keep no copy, there is nothing you need to request from us and nothing we
need to delete on your behalf.

## Can this policy change?

We may update it if the app's features or the applicable law change. Any revision
will be published on this website.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, write to
[support@tortugapower.com](mailto:support@tortugapower.com).

Effective date: September 16, 2026
